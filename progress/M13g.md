## What was built

- Natural spawn rules for all ten Overworld creatures, including suitable passive ground, Mossback water proximity, Glimmer Moth night/light/air conditions, zero-block-light hostiles, and mushroom-cave Sporeburst weighting.
- Fixed-budget spawning with bounded column bookkeeping, distant hostile despawn, and a default-on switch that makes no spawn-system or queue work when disabled.
- Passive density previously relied on one local sample every 400 ticks. It now gets a bounded first-generation population attempt for each generated column as the insertion-order queue drains over time; the one-local-sample-per-400-ticks path still adds animals later.
- Every grouped spawn now rechecks the selected rule's biome, spot, and `where` conditions for each member, including Glimmer Moth followers.
- Ceiling pursuit for Skitterers and death/respawn clearing for player entity-damage immunity.
- Updated the existing M13h Cinderwraith assertion and added unit and E2E coverage.
