## Known limitations

- Dark cube textures (stone, coal) read less clearly than bright ones at 16×16.
- Q on a hovered slot while the cursor holds a stack drops from the slot, not the cursor.
- The unstackable swap fix has no visible effect with current data (identical items); the unit test checks that the stacks are exchanged.
- `progress/M09a.md`'s status line is out of date (`incomplete; … CI pending`); not edited, per AGENTS.md §8.
- The browser may refuse to re-lock the pointer after the screen closes; clicking the game then locks it.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- Open screens with `UiManager.open(name)` (extend `ScreenName` and the draw dispatch); `activeUi.manager` is the running instance. `uiScaleFor(w, h)` and the kit in `ui-kit.ts` are for M09c's HUD. Items and blocks get icons from `icons.rects` by item id or block id.
- While a screen is open `InputEngine.captured` is true; M09c's number keys and wheel must respect it.
- `getScreen().slots[i]` are CSS-pixel rects for slot `i`; crop screenshots with them.
