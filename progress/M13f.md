## What was built

- `src/entity/mobs/hollow.ts`, `thornling.ts`, `skitterer.ts`, `sporeburst.ts`: models, skins, palettes and definitions, registered with M13e's `registerCreature()`.
- `src/entity/mobs/spore-cloud.ts`: the lingering spore cloud (an entity, its model and skin, and its system).
- `src/entity/ai/goals/leap.ts` (`LeapGoal`) and `approach.ts` (`ApproachGoal`).
- `src/entity/player-slow.ts`: player slowing, kept outside the Player object.
- The thorn projectile kind and model (in `thornling.ts`), through M13d's projectile module.
- Player i-frames in `src/gameplay/combat.ts`.

## Known limitations

- No natural spawning; M13g registers it (the data is in the decisions file).
- The Hollow does not break doors "on hard difficulty", because there is no difficulty system.
- The Skitterer climbs walls but not ceilings: M13c's movement only climbs against a horizontal collision.
- The Sporeburst burst removes no blocks (M14 routes it through explosions).
- The spore cloud only slows the player. It does not slow mobs, and there is no general status-effect system.
- No creature sounds (M16).
- Creatures are not saved.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- **M13g:** register the `SpawnRule`s from the decisions file. All four are hostile and use the spawner's existing dark-spot rule. Sporebursts should be weighted up in mushroom-heavy caves.
- **M14:** `SporeSwellGoal` (in `sporeburst.ts`) extends M13c's `SwellGoal`. Route the burst through the explosion system there, or in `SwellGoal`, and keep `spawnSporeCloud` on the last swell tick.
- **Player i-frames:** any entity damage to the player must go through `hurtPlayer`. Two hits from entities in the same tick, or within 10 ticks, deal damage once. In unit tests, run `PlayerCombat.tick()` 10 times (`PLAYER_INVULNERABLE_TICKS`) between hits.
- **Slowing the player:** call `slowPlayer(player, factor, ticks)` from `src/entity/player-slow.ts`, or `MobPlayer.slow` from AI code.
- **Mob projectiles:** register a kind with `pickup: false` and pass `pickup: false` to `fireProjectile` too (see `fireThorn`).
- **Testing:** spawn yaw is random. To screenshot a creature facing the camera, respawn it until `getEntityAI(id).yaw` is near the angle you want, then let the killed ones finish dying (about 25 ticks).
