- **m04-fast-flight.png** (final verify run): taken 500 blocks into the 1000-block flight at 30 blocks/s, RD 8, with streaming not waited for. Stone cave and ravine walls and ceiling fill the upper half, with a terracotta and sand slope at the left and a little grass near the top. The lower half shows a dark stone floor in the middle, and large areas of solid sky-coloured background at the lower left, centre and right, where no terrain is drawn. `columnsFading` was 18 at capture, `meshQueued` 68, `uploadsDeferred` 174. I did not check this frame's edges for dither, so I cannot say how much of the gap is columns still fading in. The frame is a different capture from the earlier run's (the earlier one showed the same kind of gap in the middle distance), so the numbers change from run to run.

  **Why this frame does not fully meet its checklist** ("at most a few chunks still loading at the far edge, near terrain fully present"): the gaps are in the middle distance, not only at the far edge, and they are not a few chunks. The cause is the streaming speed on SwiftShader, from the M04a notes: one section upload costs 6–10 ms (`uploadMsP95` was 5.2–10.8 ms across ten flights), so a frame can upload about one section; the GPU runs at about 1.3 frames per second at RD 8 (0.4–0.8 s per frame); and drawing ~740 sections costs about 5 ms. Streaming cannot keep up with a 30 blocks/s flight here, so the frame shows the streaming state. The orchestrator decided to keep it as a known limitation for this PR. **M22a is asked to retake this frame** once draw submission and upload cost are lower.

**Hole finder on the fast-flight frame** (final verify run, numbers as measured): open sky 0 px, enclosed 100,189 px, largest component 52,274 px. (The earlier run measured 37,510 px and 32,390 px; the frame changes from run to run.) The top row has no sky, so all sky is enclosed. Enclosed sky here means sky not reached from the top row. The frame cannot say which of two things it is:
- terrain not yet drawn, or columns still fading in (the dithered edges, `meshQueued` 101, `uploadsDeferred` 171, `columnsFading` 19 at capture);
- sky beyond the loaded edge at RD 8. At this same pose, probed separately, sky was 0.60 % of the frame after streaming went idle at RD 8 and 0.07 % at RD 12. So part of the sky at idle lies beyond the RD 8 edge. The probe's frame and the test's frame are different captures of one pose, so the two numbers are not the same frame.

This is a property of the top-row seed (as specified) on a frame whose top row is terrain. It does not affect any assertion (the frame is asserted only with `assertNotBlank` and `assertNoMissingTexture`).

## What was built

- **`src/render/dither.ts`** — the 4×4 Bayer table; the same table feeds the fragment shader (`bayerGlsl()`).
- **`src/render/column-fade.ts`** — `FADE_IN_MS = 400`, `fadeAmount`, `ColumnFades`: a fade starts at the first section upload of a column with no section meshes on the GPU, and ends when its last section mesh is freed.
- **`src/render/chunk-renderer.ts`** — `u_fade` uniform and the dither discard at the top of the fragment shader (before the wireframe early return); one `uniform1f` per column per pass; `uploadSectionMesh(…, fadeFromMs)`; the wireframe overlay loops over visible columns so it follows the fades; `fadingColumns(nowMs)`.
- **`src/world/mesh-attempts.ts`** — `MeshAttempts`, the store of each open attempt's uploaded sections. An entry exists only while an attempt is open.
- **`src/world/streamer.ts`** — `StreamerHost.abandonMeshAttempt(cx, cz)`, called from `discardAttempt` when the column is `meshing`; `requestPending` getter.
- **`src/world/world-manager.ts`** — the streamer host uses `MeshAttempts`; abandon on `freeMesh` and `freeData`; the store is cleared with the GPU meshes in `loadInitialWorld` and `resetWorldToEmpty`; `uploadSection` passes no fade start while `createWorld` or a region request is pending; `getStreamingStats()` returns `WorldStreamingStats` (adds `columnsFading`).
- **`src/debug/api/core.ts`, `src/debug/api/streaming.ts`** — type updates only.
- **`tests/e2e/helpers/sky-holes.ts`** — `isSkyPixel`, `findSkyHoles` (flood fill from the top row, then the largest 4-connected enclosed component), `MAX_HOLE_PIXELS = 64`.
- **`tests/e2e/m04.spec.ts`** — the three M04b tests, with the heap probe, the pre-shot coverage and fade checks, and the hole check.

Existing tests changed: `tests/unit/streamer.test.ts`'s `FakeHost` gains `abandonMeshAttempt` (it records calls and mirrors the world manager's attempt store). No assertion was changed or removed.

## Known limitations

- **Heap readings before the launch flag.** Without `--enable-precise-memory-info`, `performance.memory` returned the same value before and after a 64 MB array (19,300,000 B), so the first heap check could not fail. My earlier note here said Chrome reports 100 KB steps. That was wrong: the readings were coarse and cached. The flag is on master (`90c809b`, SPEC §5.2 in `67381df`), and the merged tree now sees the array.
- **Heap growth is negative, and the check does not catch it.** In the three runs the end of the flight was 40–41 % below the baseline at spawn. The 15 % check is an upper bound, so a drop of that size passes. I have not found which objects are released; one guess is memory from `createWorld` that is still held at the baseline, but that is untested. If the owner wants a two-sided check, that changes the owner's decision.
- **Fast-flight frame does not meet its checklist** (see Screenshots and the reasons above). Kept as a known limitation for this PR; M22a to retake.
- **Hole finder seeds from the top row only** (as specified, kept by the orchestrator's decision). Within one pixel column, a ray that is lower than a ray hitting terrain hits terrain no farther away, but only for downward rays over a height field. The top row of the fast-flight frame looks up at a ceiling, and caves and overhangs let a lower ray pass through open air, so this argument does not show that the enclosed sky there is undrawn terrain. The orchestrator's summary "enclosed sky = terrain not yet drawn or mid-fade; no loaded edge in view" is not written here as stated, because the RD 8 / RD 12 probe above contradicts "no loaded edge in view" at that pose. The numbers stay as measured.
- **Horizon hole check** covers the whole frame, so it also looks at the middle of the frame, where the far chunks are. It does not prove that the far edge at the corners is correct, only that no enclosed hole larger than 64 px exists.
- **The fade is rarely seen under SwiftShader.** A frame takes about 0.75 s here, so a 400 ms fade usually ends before the next frame. The dither was visible in the earlier fast-flight frame at the edges of the gaps (the speckled band). The final frame was not checked for it. No e2e test checks a pixel mid-fade; the dither itself is covered by unit tests of the table.
- **A column whose generation failed three times still blocks its neighbours** (the known M04a limitation). No test here hit it.
- **Mesh failures are still silent** (M04a limitation).
- **A column whose meshes are all freed by a stale completion fades again** when it comes back: `finishIfDone`'s stale branch frees the column's meshes, so the column disappears and fades back in. That is outside decision 2 as written and is not changed here.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- **M22a** (culling and batching): retake `m04-fast-flight.png` once streaming keeps up at RD 8 (the frame in this PR is a known limitation). `ChunkRenderer.render()` sets `u_fade` per column in each pass; a batched draw must keep the fade per column (one uniform per column, or per batch of columns with equal fade).
- **M05b** (lighting): a column's fade starts at its first section upload (`uploadSection` in `world-manager.ts`). If M05b changes a column's light after that upload, the change shows during the fade, as it does for any mesh update. Nothing in M04b depends on the light data.
- **Debug API:** `getStreamingStats()` returns `WorldStreamingStats`; `columnsFading` is time-based, read at the call. A test that waits for it to reach zero should still wait two frames before a screenshot.
- **Attempts:** `StreamerHost.abandonMeshAttempt` must be implemented by any new host; the streamer calls it from `discardAttempt` only while a column is `meshing`.
- **Heap test:** `window.gc` needs `--js-flags=--expose-gc`, and the readings need `--enable-precise-memory-info`; both are in `playwright.config.ts` on the merged tree. The test throws if `window.gc` is missing. The probe checks that the reading responds to a known allocation before the flight.
