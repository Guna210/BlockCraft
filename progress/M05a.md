## Light Engine Opacity & Emission Rules

1. **Storage:**
   - Light storage is kept completely separate from `ChunkSection` in `LightStorage` (`src/world/lighting.ts`) using nibble-packed (0–15) sky and block light per 16³ section.
   - `ChunkSection` storage and `getByteSize()` remain unchanged, ensuring uniform section memory stays ≤ 64 bytes.

2. **Block Opacity & Light Attenuation:**
   - **Full Opaque Cubes:** Opacity = 15. Light cannot propagate through.
   - **Air, Glass, Stained Glass, Ice:** Opacity = 0.
   - **Leaves:** Opacity = 1 (attenuates light by 1 extra level in addition to distance decay).
   - **Water:** Opacity = 2 (attenuates light by 2 extra levels in addition to distance decay).
   - **Sky Light Vertical Rays:** Sky light at 15 travels straight down vertically without loss through 0-opacity blocks (air, glass). Once it encounters a non-zero opacity block or spreads horizontally, it attenuates by `1 + opacity` per step.

3. **Block Light Emission:**
   - Emitters draw emission directly from the block registry (e.g. Lava emission = 15).
   - Unit tests use a level-14 emitter clearly named as a torch stand-in.

4. **Edge of Loaded Columns:**
   - BFS queues check whether neighbor columns exist (`world.hasColumn(ncx, ncz)`).
   - Light propagation stops at unloaded column boundaries, preventing accidental allocation or creation of unloaded columns.
