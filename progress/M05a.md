## Documented Light Rules

1. **Full Opaque Cubes:** Blocks marked `fullOpaqueCube: true` in registry (e.g., stone, dirt, wood) have opacity 15 and completely block all light propagation.
2. **Transparent & Attenuating Blocks:**
   - Air and Glass: opacity 0. Light passes through horizontally and upward with −1 decay per step.
   - Sky Light Downward Propagation: Sky light at level 15 travels straight down through opacity 0 blocks (air and glass) without loss of light intensity (remains 15).
   - Water and Leaves: opacity 2 (attenuates light by 2 per block step).
   - Other non-opaque blocks default to opacity 1.
3. **Emitters:**
   - Lava emission (15) comes directly from the real registry `lightEmission` definition.
   - Torch emission (14) belongs to M05b; unit tests construct their own emission lookup table with a level-14 emitter named as a torch stand-in.
4. **Loaded Column Boundaries:**
   - At the edge of loaded columns, light propagation BFS strictly checks `world.hasColumn(cx, cz)`.
   - Light never spreads into unloaded columns, preventing premature allocation or creation of neighbor columns.

## What was built

- `src/world/lighting.ts`:
  - `LightStorage`: Section-keyed map storing nibble-packed (4-bit block light, 4-bit sky light) `Uint8Array(4096)` buffers outside `ChunkSection` to ensure `ChunkSection.getByteSize()` remains unaffected and `chunk.test.ts` passes unchanged.
  - `buildLightLookupTables`: Builds flat `opacity` and `emission` typed arrays from `BlockRegistry` (supporting custom test emission overrides).
  - `LightEngine`: Preallocated typed-array queues (`addX`, `addY`, `addZ`, `removeX`, `removeY`, `removeZ`, `removeVal`) for BFS add and remove two-queue algorithms with zero inner-loop heap allocations. Tracks touched sections via `getTouchedSectionsCount()`.
- `src/workers/light.worker.ts` & `src/workers/light-worker-pool.ts`:
  - Web Worker and main-thread `LightWorkerPool` for offloading bulk initial light propagation of chunk regions using transferable ArrayBuffers.
- `src/debug/api/light.ts`:
  - Implemented `getLight(x, y, z)` returning real engine values `{ sky: number; block: number }`. Registered in `src/debug/api/index.ts`.
- `tests/unit/lighting.test.ts`:
  - Comprehensive unit test suite exercising all five owned acceptance criteria, loaded column boundary checks, opacity/attenuation rules, and bulk propagation vs incremental edits equality.
