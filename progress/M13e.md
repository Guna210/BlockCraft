## What was built

- `src/entity/mobs/`:
  - `tuftbuck.ts`, `rootboar.ts`, `dapplefowl.ts`, `mossback.ts`, `glimmer-moth.ts`: model, skin, palette, definition and registration of each creature.
  - `creature.ts`: `registerCreature`, `spawnCreature`, babies (`GROW_UP_TICKS`, `feedBaby`, `growUp`), death drops, and the creature system, added lazily per ECS world.
  - `kit.ts`: `buildModel` (automatic UV packing) and `SkinPainter` (flat banded shades of a ramp; sparse clumps and strands, spots and fringe as deliberate shapes).
  - `index.ts`: imports the five.
- `src/entity/ai/goals/`:
  - `forage.ts`: graze and root.
  - `lay-egg.ts`, `withdraw.ts`, `amphibious-wander.ts`.
- `src/entity/ai/interactions.ts`: `'use'` handlers per mob type (shearing, feeding babies).

## Known limitations

- **No natural spawning:** the orchestrator moved it to M13g during this task, because registered rules broke other milestones' e2e tests (m07b:106, m08c:160, m17b:70). Creatures appear only through `spawn` and breeding. The intended spawn data is in the decisions record under "Natural spawning: for M13g".
- Creatures are not saved (creature persistence is out of scope).
- The Tuftbuck's fleece colour is fixed at spawn from its biome. A baby takes the fleece of the biome it is born in.
- No creature sounds (M16b).

## Notes for dependent tasks

- **New creature (M13f, M20):**
  - Write a `CreatureDefinition` and call `registerCreature({definition, models})` in `src/entity/mobs/<name>.ts`, then import it from `src/entity/mobs/index.ts`.
  - Build models with `buildModel` (no hand-written UVs) and paint skins with `SkinPainter`.
- **`PRIORITY`** in `creature.ts` is the shared goal ranking.
- **Death drops:** `definition.drops(ctx, id)` is rolled once on the first dying tick. Use `ctx.random` for counts.
- **`'use'` on a mob:** `definition.interact` returns `'consume'`, `'wear'` or `null`.
- **Glow:** set `emissive: true` on a model to draw it full-bright.
- **M13g:** register the spawn rules from the decisions record with M13c's `registerSpawnRule`. With rules registered, flat (plains) test worlds fill with Tuftbucks and Dapplefowl. That breaks `m07b.spec.ts:106`, and at times `m08c.spec.ts:160` and `m17b.spec.ts:70`. M13c's passive spots also need grass (no Mossbacks on sand) and its ambient spots have no light or time test (needed for the moth).
- **Invariant:** the creature system is added to an ECS world by its first creature. Keep it that way so worlds without creatures pay nothing.
- **Testing with creatures nearby:** in e2e tests, kill the other creatures, tick once, then kill the item entities before the player can pick them up. Otherwise the player's inventory can hold their drops (for example `raw_fowl` in slot 0).
