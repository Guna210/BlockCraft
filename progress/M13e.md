## Known limitations

- Creatures are not saved (creature persistence is out of scope). After a reload, passives respawn only on newly generated columns and through the rare 400-tick attempts.
- The Tuftbuck's fleece colour is fixed at spawn from its biome. A baby takes the fleece of the biome it is born in.
- No creature sounds (M16b).
- Natural spawning is registered but not verified end to end (M13g).
- With `type: 'flat'` worlds now holding Tuftbucks and Dapplefowl, e2e tests that need an empty area should remove the natural mobs and their drops first, as `tests/e2e/m13e.spec.ts` does.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- **New creature (M13f, M20):**
  - Write a `CreatureDefinition` and call `registerCreature({definition, models, spawn})` in `src/entity/mobs/<name>.ts`, then import it from `src/entity/mobs/index.ts`.
  - Build models with `buildModel` (no hand-written UVs) and paint skins with `SkinPainter`.
- **`PRIORITY`** in `creature.ts` is the shared goal ranking.
- **Death drops:** `definition.drops(ctx, id)` is rolled once on the first dying tick. Use `ctx.random` for counts.
- **`'use'` on a mob:** `definition.interact` returns `'consume'`, `'wear'` or `null`.
- **Glow:** set `emissive: true` on a model to draw it full-bright.
- **Spawn rules:** `ground` (passive spawn blocks) and `where(world, x, y, z)` (extra spot test).
- **Invariant:** the creature system is added to an ECS world by its first creature. Keep it that way so worlds without creatures pay nothing.
- **Testing on flat worlds:** in e2e tests, kill natural creatures, tick once, then kill the item entities before the player can pick them up. Otherwise the player's inventory can hold their drops (for example `raw_fowl` in slot 0).
