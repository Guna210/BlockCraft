## Leaf Tinting Decision

- **Oak leaves (`oak_leaves`):** Tinted with the biome foliage tint (`tintIndex = 2`). Oak forests shift color smoothly from bright green in plains/meadows to deep emerald in rainforests and dark olive in swamps.
- **Birch leaves (`birch_leaves`) & Pine leaves (`pine_leaves`):** Fixed green tiles (`tintIndex = 0`). Keeping birch and pine leaves untinted preserves their signature pale birch-yellow/light-green and dark evergreen-spruce foliage identities across all biomes.

## What was built

- `data/blocks/biome-surface.json`: Registered `red_sand`, `clay`, `snow`, `mire`, and 8 Clayrock colors (`clayrock_white`, `clayrock_light_gray`, `clayrock_yellow`, `clayrock_orange`, `clayrock_terracotta`, `clayrock_red`, `clayrock_brown`, `clayrock_dark_brown`).
- `src/render/textures/biome-surface.ts` & `src/render/textures/index.ts`: Built procedural generators and palettes for new blocks adhering strictly to ART.md rules (all palettes <= 12 colors, luminance contrast >= 60, seamless wrapping). Registered generators in `textureGenerators`, `texturePalettes`, and `M01_REQUIRED_TEXTURES`.
- `src/gen/biomes.ts`: Defined all 19 Overworld biomes with temperature, humidity, grass tint, foliage tint, and surface rules. Implemented allocation-free `sampleBiome(worldSeed, wx, wz)`.
- `src/gen/surface.ts`: Implemented `biomeSurfaceStage` applying biome surface rules (grass+dirt, sand, clayrock strata, mire, snow, gravel, stone cliffs, river bed clay/sand/gravel) to the top layers of terrain shape. Smoothly blended grass and foliage tints across 13×13 pre-sampled block neighborhoods.
- `src/gen/pipeline.ts`: Inserted `biomeSurfaceStage` into `createDefaultPipeline()` in one line right after `terrainShapeStage`.
- `src/world/column.ts`: Stored per-column biome IDs (`Uint8Array(256)`) and grass/foliage tint maps (`Uint8Array`) on `ChunkColumn`, leaving `src/world/chunk.ts` unchanged.
- `src/world/world.ts`: Updated `getBiome(x, z)` to return column biome for loaded columns and `sampleBiome` otherwise (and always plains in flat worlds).
- `src/workers/gen.worker.ts` & `src/workers/gen-worker-pool.ts`: Transferred biome indices (`Uint8Array`), grass tints (`Uint8Array`), and foliage tints (`Uint8Array`) as transferable buffers alongside section states.
- `src/mesh/greedy.ts`: Assigned `tintIndex` per block face (1 for grass_block top, 2 for oak_leaves, 3 for grass_side, 0 for none) in `MeshLookupTables`, included `tintIdx` in face merge key, and packed `tintIndex` into Word 0.
- `src/render/chunk-renderer.ts` & GLSL shaders: Uploaded and sampled per-column grass and foliage tints in GLSL. Multiplied neutral tiles by sampled tints, detecting `grass_side` top fringe vs untinted brown dirt base. Defaulted flat worlds and test scenes to Plains tint.
- `src/render/test-scene.ts` & `tests/unit/texture-sheet.test.ts`: Applied Plains grass and foliage tints to `grass_top`, `grass_side` fringe, and `oak_leaves` in test scene rendering and texture sheet composition.
- `src/debug/api/locate.ts`: Built `locate(kind, id, near)` dispatcher module for `'biome'` spiral searches returning `[x, ySurface + 1, z]`, returning `null` for unregistered kinds.
- `src/debug/api/world.ts` & `src/debug/api/index.ts`: Exposed `getBiome` and `locate` debug API methods.
- `decisions/M03c-biome-tint.md`: Documented tinting mechanism and mip-chain mask handling.
- `tests/unit/terrain.test.ts`: Isolated `terrainShapeStage` for M03b material assertion test.
- `tests/unit/biomes.test.ts`: Added unit tests verifying criteria 9a–9f and generating 1024×1024 top-down maps `m03c-biome-map.png` and `m03c-grass-tint-map.png`.
- `tests/e2e/m03c.spec.ts`: Added E2E tests verifying grass tint rendering in Savanna vs Rainforest with channel diff 34.1 ≥ 20, M01c test scene grass top face green hue (mean hue 80–140°), zero missing textures, and saved screenshots to `docs/screenshots/M03c/`.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- **`locate` Dispatcher (for M03e, M19a):**
  - `src/debug/api/locate.ts` exports `registerLocator(kind, locatorFn)` and `locate(kind, id, near)`.
  - Unregistered kinds return `null`.
  - M03e registers `'ore'` in `src/debug/api/locate.ts` via `registerLocator('ore', locateOre)`.
- **Biome Storage & Transfer:**
  - `ChunkColumn` exposes `biomes` (`string[]` of 256 biome IDs), `biomeIndices` (`Uint8Array(256)` indices into `OVERWORLD_BIOME_IDS`), and `grassTints`/`foliageTints` (`Uint8Array` of 256*3 RGB bytes).
  - Transferred from `gen.worker.ts` as `Uint8Array` transferable buffers.
  - To map a biome index byte to a string ID, import `OVERWORLD_BIOME_IDS` from `src/gen/biomes.ts`: `OVERWORLD_BIOME_IDS[index]`.
  - `World.getBiome(x, z)` returns stored column biome if column is loaded, or `sampleBiome(worldSeed, x, z)` otherwise.
