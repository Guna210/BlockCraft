## Known limitations

- Known 2-core container failure not caused by this task: m04.spec.ts:276 (RD12).
- Stained glass recipes were added (8 glass around a colourant) because the M10a reachability test requires every item to be obtainable and no dyes exist.
- Packed ice is now a translucent, non-opaque block with light opacity 3 (SPEC places it in the translucent pass).
- Wind uses its own clock (`windAnimation`) that freezes on `setFluidAnimationPaused(true)` or `api.pause()`, deviating from "same clock as setFluidAnimationPaused": the unchanged M12c clear-weather control only pauses the simulation. No existing test was edited.
- While remeshing after a leaves change, old fancy meshes briefly draw with the fast fragment path.
