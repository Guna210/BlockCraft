## What was built

- Worker-computed section face masks, including compact worker results for sections the streamer can skip; renderer-owned mask lifetime and invalidation.
- Reusable monotonic cave traversal, hierarchical frustum filtering and debug-only culling switches.
- Per-pass paged GPU arenas with range allocation and release, multi-draw when available and per-section fallback. Per-draw origins and fades are carried in a uniform array; sorted translucent draws retain their order.
- New unit tests for masks, traversal, arena allocation and GPU page release, translucent sorting and draw-call measurements. New browser tests cover culling, fallback, flight budgets and heap growth.

## Code of other tasks touched

- M02 meshing: `src/mesh/greedy.ts`, `src/mesh/worker-pool.ts` and `src/workers/mesh.worker.ts` carry worker-computed masks, including masks for streamer-skipped sections.
- M04 streaming and rendering: `src/game/world-manager.ts` invalidates and retains masks with mesh attempts, records paired worker times and passes the active render distance. `src/render/chunk-renderer.ts` owns culling, arena-backed draws, fades and page lifetime. `src/world/frustum.ts` avoids a per-call closure.

- Debug and timing: `src/debug/api/core.ts`, `src/debug/api/index.ts`, `src/engine/frame-stats.ts` and `src/main.ts` expose the debug-only controls and measure worker samples and peak draw calls.
