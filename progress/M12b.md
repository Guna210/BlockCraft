## What was built

- **Procedural Chunky Cloud Generator & Renderer (`src/render/clouds.ts`):**
  - Generated using harmonic lower-frequency periodic noise: 4-cell + 2-cell wavelength octaves (periods width/4 and width/2) combined as `v1 * 0.8 + v2 * 0.2`.
  - Applied 2×2 morphological opening (erode then dilate with periodic grid wrapping), mathematically ensuring every cell in a cluster >= 4 belongs to a 2×2 block (`strandViolations = 0`) and removing all thin 1-cell arms, corridors, and maze-like L-shapes.
  - Scattered isolated lone cells and 2–3 cell clumps added at isolated noise peaks.
  - Zero interior holes. Coverage is 31.3% (721 active cells of 2304 for seed 12006).
  - Extruded 3D volumes (3 blocks thick, y=192..195) with directional face shading:
    - Top face: white (`1.0`)
    - Vertical sides: near-white (`0.90, 0.92, 0.95`, shade `0.96`), softening the side-to-underside contrast while preserving the bright highlight rim
    - Underside: flat pale lavender-blue (`0.78` base), tinted toward sky colour (`mix(vec3(0.72, 0.74, 0.82), skyCol, 0.30)`)
  - Dusk and night lighting:
    - Sunrise/sunset: warm horizon/sunset colour (orange-pink tint, lighter where the sun is):
      `sunsetCol = mix(vec3(0.85, 0.52, 0.42), vec3(0.98, 0.72, 0.55), sunAzimuth) * (v_shade * 0.35 + 0.65)` driven by `u_twilightFactor * 0.85`.
    - Night: dim blue-grey `vec3(0.12, 0.15, 0.22) * v_shade` scaled by `(0.20 + 0.80 * u_skyBrightness)`.
  - Opaque rendering: `gl.enable(gl.DEPTH_TEST)`, `gl.depthFunc(gl.LEQUAL)`, `gl.depthMask(true)`, `gl.disable(gl.BLEND)`, `fragColor = vec4(finalRgb, 1.0)`. Clouds are fully solid and hide the sun disc and glare behind them with zero translucency.
  - Gentle 50% horizon fade: `cloudRange = Math.max(512, 2 * terrainFogEnd)`, `fogStart = cloudRange * 0.50`, `fogEnd = cloudRange`. Smoothly blends into the sky dome before reaching `fogEnd`, leaving no visible boundary line. Fancy triangles are now 12,643 after greedy-merging the mesh.
  - Fast 2D flat textured plane at y=192 using a 32×32 nearest-neighbor repeating texture.
  - Wind drift advancing smoothly along +X at 1.0 block/s derived from `worldTime / 20.0`.
  - Static GPU VBO/VAO buffers created at init time; zero per-frame buffer allocations. Tiled regions dynamically bounded by `radius = Math.ceil(fogEnd / regSize)`.
- **Engine Settings & Debug API (`src/engine/settings.ts`, `src/debug/api/clouds.ts`, `src/debug/api/core.ts`, `src/debug/api/index.ts`):**
  - Added `clouds: 'fancy' | 'fast' | 'off'` (default `'fancy'`) to engine settings.
  - Added `setClouds(mode)` to debug API and registered in `src/debug/api/index.ts`.
- **Sun Glare in Sky Shader (`src/render/sky.ts`):**
  - Height-dependent sun glare calculated in `SKY_FS` based on view ray alignment with `u_sunDir` and sun altitude `u_sunDir.y`.
  - High sun: large radius, bright white halo washing out billboard disc.
  - Low sun: shrunken radius, dimmed warm amber halo, crisp legible pixel disc.
  - Opaque clouds depth-test and overwrite the sky dome, completely blocking glare where clouds pass in front of the sun.
