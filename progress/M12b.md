## What was built

- **Procedural Cloud Generator & Renderer (`src/render/clouds.ts`):**
  - Seeded 32×32 periodic cellular noise grid generator using harmonic multi-octave noise, dual-offset notch carving waves, cellular jitter, and an interior hole punching pass. Produces lone cells, small clumps, and larger ragged clusters with holes and notches; strictly 35% coverage (358 cells).
  - Extruded 3D volumes (4 blocks thick, y=192..196) with face culling between adjacent cells, directional face shading:
    - Top face: white (`1.0`)
    - Vertical sides: near-white (`0.96`), creating a thin bright rim along all edges and holes
    - Underside: flat pale blue-grey (`0.78` base), subtly tinted toward sky colour (`mix(vec3(0.76, 0.77, 0.80), skyCol, 0.12)`)
  - Opaque rendering: `gl.enable(gl.DEPTH_TEST)`, `gl.depthFunc(gl.LEQUAL)`, `gl.depthMask(true)`, `gl.disable(gl.BLEND)`, `fragColor = vec4(finalRgb, 1.0)`. Clouds are fully solid, hide the sun disc and glare behind them, with zero translucency.
  - Wide cloud range independent of terrain fog: `cloudRange = Math.max(768, 2 * (renderDistance - 1) * 16)`. Reaches visibly to the horizon.
  - Far-away fade: horizontal distance `length(worldPos.xz - cameraPos.xz)` ensures zero fade overhead, near camera, or across screen edges. Clouds remain 100% solid until the outer ~30% (`0.70 * cloudRange`), where they seamlessly blend RGB into `skyAtmosphereColor`. Discard at `dist >= cloudRange`.
  - Fast 2D flat textured plane at y=192 using a 32×32 nearest-neighbor repeating texture generated from the noise grid.
  - Wind drift advancing smoothly along +X at 1.0 block/s derived from `worldTime / 20.0`.
  - Static GPU VBO/VAO buffers created at init time; zero per-frame buffer allocations. Tiled regions dynamically bounded by `radius = Math.ceil(fogEnd / regSize)`.
- **Engine Settings & Debug API (`src/engine/settings.ts`, `src/debug/api/clouds.ts`, `src/debug/api/core.ts`, `src/debug/api/index.ts`):**
  - Added `clouds: 'fancy' | 'fast' | 'off'` (default `'fancy'`) to engine settings.
  - Added `setClouds(mode)` to debug API and registered in `src/debug/api/index.ts`.
- **Sun Glare in Sky Shader (`src/render/sky.ts`):**
  - Height-dependent sun glare calculated in `SKY_FS` based on view ray alignment with `u_sunDir` and sun altitude `u_sunDir.y`.
  - High sun: large radius, bright white halo washing out billboard disc.
  - Low sun: shrunken radius, dimmed warm amber halo, crisp legible pixel disc.
  - Opaque clouds depth-test and overwrite the sky dome, completely blocking glare where clouds pass in front of the sun.
