## What was built

- `src/gen/caves.ts`: Implemented `generateCaves` as `caveStage`. Evaluates 3D cheese noise, 3D spaghetti noise channels, and deterministic worm carvers (evaluating worms from a 5×5 chunk neighborhood per column). Enforces safety rules: Foundation Stone and $y \le 4$ protection, 3-block safety buffer below surface water, surface protection (entrances only allowed where entrance noise $\ge 0.65$), aquifers filled with water up to local water level with solid barrier checks to ensure water never touches cave air horizontally, and lava lakes filling carved air below $y=12$. Operates allocation-free per block in workers.
- `src/gen/pipeline.ts`: Added `pipeline.addStage(caveStage);` directly after `pipeline.addStage(biomeSurfaceStage);`.
- `tests/unit/caves.test.ts`: Added unit tests verifying criteria 5a–5e and 2a–2e: cave air % per 10-block y band, largest 6-connected component for both seeds, seamlessness across generation orders, border continuity, and safety rules (zero water touching air horizontally) across 100 columns per seed.
- `tests/e2e/m03d.spec.ts`: Added e2e test that locates a large underground cave, positions camera inside, verifies camera position and head blocks are air, captures screenshot to `artifacts/m03/m03d-cave.png`, copies it to `docs/screenshots/M03d/m03d-cave.png`, and asserts `assertNotBlank` and `assertNoMissingTexture`.
