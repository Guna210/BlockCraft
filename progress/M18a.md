## What was built

- **Main menu** (`src/ui/main-menu.ts`):
  - code-drawn logo (`src/ui/logo.ts`);
  - 25 original splashes (`data/splashes.json`), yellow, pulsing, at −20°;
  - Singleplayer, which opens the M17c world list, and Credits (with Back).
- **Live panorama:**
  - seed `blockcraft-panorama` at render distance 4, using the normal renderer and workers;
  - one camera turn per 120 s;
  - starts 1 s after the menu shows and fades in over 1.5 s;
  - fully torn down when a world starts (`WorldManager.startPanorama` / `stopPanorama` / `leaveMenu`).
- **Create World screen** on the world-list canvas:
  - name (default "New World", M17c rules, a clash shows a message);
  - free-text seed (empty means random, and the seed is shown afterwards);
  - mode, type (Default/Flat/Amplified/Single Biome), a biome picker for Single Biome, Structures (stored only) and Starting Chest.
- **World types** (`src/gen/world-options.ts`):
  - Amplified (about ×2 peaks, clamped to y 319);
  - Single Biome (one biome everywhere);
  - the type and biome travel with every gen worker job.
- **Saving:** `WorldRecord.worldType` is widened, and the record gains `options`. Old records load with the defaults.
- **Starting chest** (`src/gameplay/starting-chest.ts`): 8 oak_log, 4 bread, 1 wooden_pickaxe, 1 wooden_axe and 16 torch.
- **Pause menu** (`src/ui/ui-manager.ts`):
  - Esc, or losing pointer lock, opens it, with Resume and Save & Quit;
  - Save & Quit saves, disposes the world and returns to the main menu;
  - the panorama restarts.
- **Debug API:** `createWorld` gains the optional `type: 'amplified' | 'single_biome'`, `biome`, `structures` and `startingChest` options.

## Known limitations

- **No Settings button** on the main menu or in the pause menu, per the owner's decision. M18b adds settings.
- **The Structures toggle is stored only.** Generation doesn't read it yet.
- **The panorama shows a fixed spot of the panorama seed** around (8, 8): snowy slopes and ocean.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- **Menu frames:**
  - `MainMenu.needsFrame` and `invalidate()`: anything that changes the menu's look must call `invalidate()`, or the idle menu keeps presenting the old frame.
  - Menu and panorama frames are excluded from `getRenderStats` timings, frame stats and thumbnails.
- **Panorama ownership:** while it runs, `WorldManager.panorama` is true and `WorldManager.world` is the panorama world. It is not the debug world instance.
- **Terrain variant:** read it with `terrainVariant()` from `src/gen/world-options.ts`. New generation stages that depend on the world type should read it there rather than taking new parameters.
- **Pause screen:** `UiManager` screen `'pause'`. `getScreen()` exposes `resumeButton` and `saveQuitButton` as CSS rects. `ui.onSaveAndQuit` is wired in `main.ts`.
- **Create World screen:** its Cancel and Create buttons sit at the M17c prompt positions, (690, 479) and (875, 479) CSS at 1280×720.
- **No new block ids:** `data/block-state-ids.json` is unchanged.
