## What was built

- The streaming plan caps the full-detail base radius at 12 in local Overworlds at RD13 and above. It keeps the full-detail neighbour perimeter and retention offsets from M04a.
- Worker-built heightmap tiles use a shared buffer and a dedicated shaded, fogged terrain pass. The column mask hands each column to full detail on its first completed draw and returns it to LOD when it leaves the detail ring or unloads. Tile uploads share the terrain upload deadline.
- The loading overlay uses the existing UI kit and pixel font. Its count follows the existing initial worker mesh and upload barrier, including the saved-world path.
- Debug mode exposes the tile count, visible tiles, GL submissions, pending jobs, coverage radius and full-detail radius, plus the A/B toggle.

## Known limitations

- The LOD omits trees, structures, cave openings and player edits. Coastlines and biome boundaries use the four-block sampling grid. The change from translucent voxel water to the opaque distant water surface is visible across the left side of the bay.
- Hollowdeep and multiplayer use full-detail terrain. Multiplayer join keeps its existing loading flow.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- M04/M05/M17/M18 served by `world-manager.ts` and `streamer.ts` share the existing initial-load barrier and upload deadline. LOD keeps its tile jobs and GPU data outside `World` and `Player`.
- The M02/M04/M12/M22a terrain pass receives the LOD draw after opaque terrain. It shares terrain atmosphere values and accounts for represented tile draws separately from GL submissions. The cave visibility cache also keys on FOV and aspect.
- The debug API can query `getLodStats().coverageRadius` to wait for the current tile plan to finish. It returns zero while the plan or uploads are pending.
