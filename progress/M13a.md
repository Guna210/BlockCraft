## Known limitations

- No real creature models or skins are registered (M13e, M13f). `spawn` accepts only `item:<itemId>` until they register types.
- ECS entities have no physics or AI yet (M13c); they move only when a system moves them.
- ECS entities are not saved.
- The player model (`render/player-model.ts`) still uses its own renderer; see the decision record for how it could share this one.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- Register a creature with `registerEntityModel(model, skin)` and `registerEntityType('<type>', (ctx, x, y, z) => spawnModelEntity(ctx.ecs, {...}))`. Model space faces +Z, right is −X, units are pixels; parents before children; ≤ 24 parts.
- Add AI, physics and combat systems with `ecs.addSystem(fn, order)` where −1000 < order < 1000, so the snapshot runs before them and the walk animation measures their movement.
- Use `damageEntity` for hits (it starts the 10-tick red tint; invulnerability itself is M13c's) and set a `LookTargetC` target for head tracking. `playClip(ecs, id, name)` starts a model clip.
- `PlayerController.ecs` is the live instance; `getEntities` reports ECS entities as `{ id, type, pos, health, state: 'alive' | 'dying' }`.
- Dying entities are excluded from `EntityQuery`, so the bed's hostile check and pressure plates ignore them.
