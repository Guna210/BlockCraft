## What was built

- Piston, Sticky Piston, Piston Head, Sticky Piston Head, Piston Moving, Dispenser, Note Block (104 new block states, 699–802, appended).
- `src/gameplay/mechanics/` (`Mechanics`, reached as `world.current.mechanics`): receivers evaluated by M15a's `CurrentEngine` after `settle()`. Only receivers near a changed block are re-evaluated (dirty set).
- Piston push limit 12, immovable rules, sticky pull, moving blocks as a block entity with deterministic `piston_land` scheduled ticks (2 ticks), drawn smoothly by `src/render/moving-blocks.ts`.
- Powered Doors/Trapdoors via `setOpenable` in `shape-blocks.ts`.
- Dispenser: nine-slot container, ejects through `world.itemSpawner`. Note Block: emits tone events.
- 11 texture generators (`src/render/textures/mechanics.ts`), items, icons, recipes, loot.
- Debug API `src/debug/api/mechanics.ts`: useBlock, getMovingBlocks, loadDispenser, getTones.

## Known limitations

- The Visual Review wording "door open with pistons extended" cannot be built: a head fills the cell the push moved into. The test door is closed when the sticky pistons extend and open when they retract (see decisions file).
- No dispenser screen: using a dispenser loads one held item. Breaking a dispenser does not spill its contents.
- Door edge memory, receiver registration and in-flight moves are not persisted.
- Entities are not pushed.
- `getTones` starts recording on its first call.
- Pistons, dispensers and note blocks are opaque and conduct strong power to neighbours (M15a rules).
- Changed existing test: `tests/unit/m10a-reachability.test.ts` gains exemptions for `piston_head`, `sticky_piston_head`, `piston_moving` (technical blocks, no recipe). No assertion was weakened.
