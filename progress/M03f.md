## What was built

- `src/gen/features.ts` (new): `featureStage` (stage name `features`) and the shapes of the five tree families (oak, birch, pine small / 2 × 2, rainwood small / 2 × 2, acacia), boulders (stone, cobblestone, mossy cobblestone) and ice spikes (packed ice), plants, cacti, sugar reeds, pumpkins and mushrooms.
  - Origins are one jittered point per 4 × 4 cell, hashed from world seed and cell, with an 8-block biome lattice for density and type and a rank-based clearance between neighbours. Every column evaluates all origins whose reach box touches it, so no column depends on another being generated. `src/gen/ground.ts` predicts the ground (top height, block, snow, slope) at an origin with pure functions equal to the first three stages (`probeGround`; a drift test compares it with generated columns).
  - Placement order: logs, boulders and ice first, then leaves in rank order, then plants. Only air is replaced (a log may replace a leaf). Trunks, boulders and ice need an opaque block below. Trees, boulders and spikes are not placed where the cave-entrance noise is high; plants only in an air cell above the surface, never in or over water (reeds stand with their base at y = 65 beside water).
  - Flowers and tall grass thin out in clearings (low-frequency noise, `clearingNoise`).
  - Plants have the priority pumpkin > mushroom > flower (8 × 8 patches of one kind) > tall grass; cacti on desert sand; cave mushrooms in dark underground spots.
  - No `Math.sin` / `Math.cos`, no `Math.random` (a test scans the file); all hashing is integer.
- `src/gen/pipeline.ts`: the import and `pipeline.addStage(featureStage);` only.
- Floating-block fix at its source (owner decision, option 2): `src/gen/terrain.ts`, `src/gen/caves.ts`, `src/gen/terrain-height.ts`. See `decisions/M03f-floating-blocks.md`. **Why M03f touched M03b's and M03d's code:** the owner-owned criterion "no floating single terrain blocks" could not be met by a feature stage because the isolated blocks were already produced by `terrain_shape` (3 on the alt seed) and `caves` (39 in 384 columns). The owner lifted the do-not-touch rule for those two files for this purpose only; nothing else in them changed behaviour (with the rule off, the world hash is the same as master's `25e0710b`).
- Cross models (`src/mesh/models.ts`), the separate `models` bucket in `SectionMeshData`, the cutout-pass merge in `chunk-renderer.ts`, the worker transfer; see `decisions/M03f-cross-models-and-tint.md`.
- 24 blocks and tiles in `data/blocks/wood-extra.json`, `vegetation.json`, `stone-extra.json`, generators in `src/render/textures/vegetation.ts` and `wood-extra.ts`, registered in `src/render/textures/index.ts`. No items or icons.
- `tests/unit/helpers/world-samples.ts`: shared sampling helpers (`findBiomeSpot`, `findFloating`, …) for the two new test files.
- `tests/e2e/m03f.spec.ts`: three screenshots (each test < 60 s: 7.7 s, 5.2 s, 6.0 s).

## Known limitations

- The cave stage reads cross-border neighbours through the neighbouring column's generation in about 2–2.5 % of columns (exact classification covers the rest).
- The biome lattice for tree density is 8 blocks, so a biome border is blurred by up to about 8 blocks (trees can stand in a neighbour biome within that margin: see the badlands row).
- Leaf blocks at a crown's edge can stand alone (probe: 6 and 18 isolated leaf voxels in 800 columns on the two seeds); leaves are not terrain, test 8a does not count them, and there is no leaf decay in this task.
- Ice spikes may stand on grass or stone beside snow (the 8c check allows snow, grass_block, dirt, stone).
- The "except the base of sugar reeds" exception for 8e is unused: reed bases stand at y = 65.
- Tool and `minTier` fields exist only on the new blocks (SPEC A.3 values); existing blocks are unchanged.
- Existing rendering artefacts seen in the screenshots (dark triangles on terraces and slopes, black triangular stray shapes on stone) were not investigated here (not caused by this task's blocks as far as I could see: they also appear on bare terrain far from any feature); not touched.
- The e2e world-creation slowdown described above.
- No icons or items (per task); crops, berry bushes, saplings and apple drops are not built.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- **M03e (ore stage):** add your stage between the two M03f lines in `src/gen/pipeline.ts`:
  ```ts
  pipeline.addStage(caveStage);
  // <- M03e: pipeline.addStage(oreStage);
  pipeline.addStage(featureStage);
  ```
  with `import { oreStage } from './ores';` after the `caveStage` import. The feature stage reads the earlier stages only through `probeGround` (a pure prediction of terrain, surface and caves) and the column's own blocks, and places features only on air, so ores that replace stone below the surface do not interact with it. Ores must not change blocks at or above the surface (the stage assumes the surface block it predicted, and a drift test checks it).
- **Terrain changes after M03f** (a new terrain or surface rule) must also be reflected in `src/gen/ground.ts` (`probeGround`); `tests/unit/features.test.ts` › "probeGround agrees with the generated columns" fails if they drift.
- **M03g (screenshots):** good feature spots for the standard seed, found by `locate('biome', …)` or from the probes: oakwood forest near (416, 544); rainforest near (−192, −224) (2 × 2 rainwood at (−1559, 69, 1804)); pine taiga near (−224, 288) with a 2 × 2 pine at (−1992, 68, −122); desert near (992, −512) for cacti; snowy tundra near (−224, 192) for ice spikes; savanna near (−96, −128) for acacia; meadow near (−1440, 1056) for all eight flowers; stony_heights near (544, 384) for boulders; swampland near (224, 96).
