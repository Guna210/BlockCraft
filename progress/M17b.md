## What was built

- `src/persist/idb.ts`: the IndexedDB wrapper (five stores, grouped transactions, key-prefix reads, `deleteWorld`).
- `src/persist/world-store.ts`: in-memory index of a world's saved columns over the database, region records, readers that apply the migration hook.
- `src/persist/save-manager.ts`: creating, loading and attaching worlds; the sliced incremental autosave; saves on pause, hidden page, `pagehide`, exit; capture on column unload; stats.
- `src/persist/restore.ts`: puts a saved column, block entities, ticks and door power back into a world; world, player and ground-item records.
- `src/debug/api/persist.ts`: `save`, `loadWorld`, `autosaveNow`, `getSaveStats`, `getBlockEntity`.
- `World.modifiedColumns`, `BlockEntities.restore/getInColumn`, `BlockTicks.addRebased`, `Mechanics.adopt/landAll/poweredOpenables/restorePoweredOpenables`, `CurrentEngine.restoreColumn/settleRestored`, `TickLoop.onPause`, `Player.spawnPoint`.
- `WorldManager`: saved columns come through `requestColumn`; `createWorld`, `loadWorld`, `exitWorld`, `save`.
- Player data now carries `xpLevel`, `xpProgress`, `spawnPoint`; world metadata weather fields are required.

## Known limitations

- Only named worlds are saved; there is no world list UI (M17c).
- Ticks of an unloaded column are frozen while it is unloaded and resume with their remaining delay.
- `lastRunMaxSliceMs` is wall-clock time of each synchronous stretch of the save. On a 2-core container it can include preemption or garbage collection, so the autosave-spike e2e is the test most exposed to noise. Locally it passed 12 of 12 repeated runs after the last change (it failed in about 1 of 10 runs with 144 columns and a cold first run, before the warm-up and the smaller column count).
- Dimension keys exist, but only `overworld` is saved.
