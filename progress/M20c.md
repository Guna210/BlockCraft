## What was built

- **Updrafts:**
  - `src/world/updrafts.ts` holds `UpdraftField`, a seeded cylinder per 48-block cell over
    Driftstone Expanse chasms, with O(1) cached lookups, and `gravityFactor`.
  - `src/world/world-dimension.ts` is a WeakMap dimension tag.
  - Gravity is multiplied by `gravityFactor` in `player.ts`, `ai/movement.ts`,
    `item-entity.ts` and `blast-charge.ts`.
- **Glow:** `src/render/updraft-glow.ts` draws one `POINTS` call of motes and halos, only in
  the Hollowdeep with particles on.
- **Glowwing:** `src/entity/ai/flight-path.ts` and `src/entity/mobs/glowwing.ts`.
- **Magmaw:** `src/entity/mobs/magmaw.ts`, plus `MobPlayer.ignite` in `ai/runtime.ts` and
  `engine/player-controller.ts`.
- **Spawning:**
  - `entity/spawning.ts` adds `spawnRulesFor`, the dimension parameter on `registerSpawnRule`,
    and the `SpawnRule.spot` option.
  - `entity/mobs/hollowdeep-spawn-rules.ts` holds the Hollowdeep rules.
  - `player-controller.ts` passes the world's own dimension's rules.
- **Stew:**
  - `data/recipes/hollowdeep.json` holds the recipe.
  - `gameplay/survival.ts` adds `nightVisionTicks`.
  - `persist/serializers.ts` saves an optional `nightVisionTicks`.
  - `world-manager.ts` applies the brightness floor.
- **Fog:** `u_dimensionFog` in `render/entities.ts` and `render/player-model.ts`.
- **Debug API** (`src/debug/api/hollowdeep.ts`): `getUpdraft`, `findUpdraft`,
  `getNightVision`, `getHollowdeepCreature`, `getFlightStats`, `getMusicContext` and
  `getUpdraftGlowZones`. `sound.lastMusicContext` was added in `audio/service.ts`.
- **New block ids:** none.

### Changes to code owned by other tasks

- **M06 player:** gravity is multiplied by the shared factor, which is exactly 1 outside a
  Hollowdeep Updraft.
- **M13c movement:** the same factor applies to mob gravity.
- **M07 items and M14 Blast Charges:** the same factor applies.
- **M13g spawning:**
  - The default `spawnRules` registry is unchanged.
  - Added per-dimension registries, the optional `spot` test, and a spawn height that is not
    the surface for categories whose rules all bring their own spot.
- **M11 survival / M17 persistence:** an optional `nightVisionTicks` field, absent in old
  saves.
- **M20a world manager:**
  - `attachDimension` tags the world's dimension and installs or removes the Updraft field.
  - `drawTerrain` passes the cavern fog and draws the glow.

## Known limitations

- **No translucency:** the entity renderer has only alpha cut-out. The Glowwing's
  "translucent" membrane is a pale emissive palette, not alpha blending.
- **"Falling blocks" means Blast Charges:** the game has no falling sand or gravel, so primed
  Blast Charges are the only falling-block entity.
- **Dim islands:** floating islands are nearly unlit outside the Updraft glow (M20b lighting).
- **Night vision test coverage:** the screen brightening is covered by code review and the unit
  test of the timer, with no pixel test.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- Call `gravityFactor(world, x, y, z)` for any new gravity. It costs two comparisons outside the
  Hollowdeep.
- Register Hollowdeep creatures with `registerSpawnRule(rule, 'hollowdeep')`. Never add them to
  the default `spawnRules`: M13g's unit test pins that registry's exact contents.
- The flight pathfinder is shared per world (`flightPathfinder(runtime)`). Other flyers can use
  `FlightPath` + `request`, and must call `tick(ecs.time)` once per AI tick.
- The `docs/screenshots/M20c/` spots come from seed `blockcraft-m20c` and are listed at the top
  of the e2e spec.
