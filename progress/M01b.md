## What was built
- Procedural generators for all M01 textures with deterministic seeded noise and LCG randomness, including Animated Water and Lava (32 frames each).
- Atlas packer taking 16x16 tile sizes and generating max 2 mip levels (16px, 8px, 4px) to retain padding at least 1px without subpixel bleeding, wrapping opposite tile borders to support continuous repeated wrapping.
- Unit tests confirming mip padding, rect uniqueness, texture inclusion, and generator determinism.
- Saved atlas to `artifacts/m01/m01-atlas.png`.

## Decisions (links to decisions/ files)
- [M01b-atlas.md](../../decisions/M01b-atlas.md): Mip chain vs padding choices. Decided to stop the mip chain at level 2 (`ATLAS_MAX_MIP = 2`) to maintain whole-pixel offsets and at least 1px of padding.
- Fill padding by wrapping pixels from the tile's opposite edge at every mip level, because block textures repeat across greedy-meshed faces.

## Known limitations
- RNG logic currently in `noise.ts` could be refactored to use `src/engine/rng.ts` generated by M03a later.
- Water and Lava are saved as 32 individual static tiles, the rendering system will need to manage animated coordinates per-frame using these texture rects.

## Notes for dependent tasks
- M01c will need to instantiate the `TextureAtlas` using `textureGenerators` object from `src/render/textures/index.ts`.
- Max Mip levels are generated up to 2 (16, 8, 4) in the atlas.
- Atlas tiles for fluids (water and lava) generate 32 separate frames labeled `water_0`...`water_31` and `lava_0`...`lava_31` in the atlas rect keys. M08b should index these sequential rectangles over time.
