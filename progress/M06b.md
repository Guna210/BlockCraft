## What was built

Water acceleration/drag, gentle buoyancy, surface bank exit, cached climber facing queries and Mire support queries extend M06a's single fixed-tick physics function. Camera state extends the same ticks with sprint FOV and walked-distance phase, and the controller interpolates it per frame. F5 uses rebindable `perspective` input, including the existing debug `input()` method. Solid-shape ray intersections shorten the four-block camera arm.

Ladder and vine registry entries live in the new `data/blocks/climbing.json`. Each uses a thin two-sided face through the existing model bucket. The final spare vertex bit encodes an inset from the supporting wall. Vines use biome foliage tint. No generation placement is introduced.

A six-box original player model uses procedural atlas skin and a ground-distance limb cycle, drawn between cutout and translucent terrain. Underwater camera-medium detection selects Euclidean 2–24 block water fog, a daylight-dependent minimum brightness and a final fullscreen blue blend. Above water the M12 atmosphere and horizontal distance fog stay the same. World water opacity becomes 1.
