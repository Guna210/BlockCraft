## What was built

- Three pure stages under `src/gen/hollowdeep/`: enclosed terrain, world-anchored features,
  Skyshard restricted to y 5–60 with a peak at y 20. `createHollowdeepPipeline(): TerrainPipeline` uses the existing
  pipeline contract and stage seed derivation. No new World, dimension manager or travel code.
- Duskrock floor and ceiling, Foundation Stone y 0–4, magma sea through y 40 and ceiling falls,
  floating Driftstone islands, Glowcap forests, hanging Veilroot, hollow Voidglass geodes and
  Emberite nodules near the magma shore.
- Eight blocks with textures, block items, generated icons and self-drops in new category files.
  Append-only state ids: duskrock 825, driftstone 826, glowcap_stem 827, glowcap 828,
  glowcap_gills 829, veilroot 830, voidglass 831, emberite 832.
- `HOLLOWDEEP_ENVIRONMENT` defines fog, zero sky light, floating spore settings and the 10-tick
  lava flow interval. Glowcap and gills use the existing RGB emission tint table at level 9.
