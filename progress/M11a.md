## What was built

- `src/gameplay/survival.ts`: Pure-logic survival engine modeling player health (max 20), hunger (max 20), saturation, exhaustion accumulation, air (max 300), and burning timers.
- Appendix D exhaustion accumulator: sprint (0.1/block), swim (0.01/3D block), jump (0.05 normal / 0.2 sprint-jump), block break (0.005), damage taken (0.1), natural regen (6.0 per half-heart). Subtraction of 4.0 exhaustion per saturation or hunger drop. Sprint disabled at hunger ≤ 6.
- Natural regen & starvation: Fast regen every 10 ticks at hunger 20 + saturation > 0; slow regen every 80 ticks at hunger ≥ 18; starvation every 80 ticks at hunger 0 down to floor 1 (gated to survival mode).
- Hazard damage: drowning (300 ticks air, first 2 damage at tick 320 / 20 ticks after air reaches 0, then 2 damage/20 ticks, instant refill on surfacing), lava (4 damage/10 ticks, 300 burning ticks), fire (1 damage/10 ticks, 160 burning ticks), burning status (1 damage/20 ticks, extinguished immediately on entering water), cactus AABB contact (1 damage/10 ticks), suffocation (1 damage/10 ticks inside full opaque blocks like stone; non-solid/translucent blocks like glass, leaves, slab, stairs do not suffocate), and void y < -64 (4 damage/10 ticks).
- Eating mechanics: holding 'use' for 32 ticks (1.6 s) when hunger < 20 slows movement speed by 80%, emits crumb particles with block state colors matching the food item, applies nutrition and saturation capped at hunger, and cancels on early release. Eating does not start if the same use press was consumed by block use (chest, kiln, door, workbench).
- Integration:
  - `src/entity/player.ts`: Embedded `survival: Survival`, delegated vitals accessors, clamped saturation to hunger, clamped health ≤ 0 to dead, reset partial regen timers on teleport, applied sprint disable at hunger ≤ 6, eating speed factor, jump & movement exhaustion, fall damage routing, and `burning` snapshot property.
  - `src/engine/player-controller.ts`: Survival ticked on game loop ticks; added block break exhaustion (0.005) in `onBreak`; ticked interaction prior to survival to consume use actions before eating.
  - `src/gameplay/block-interaction.ts`: Flags `survival.useConsumed = true` when block use consumes the press.
  - `src/debug/api/vitals.ts` & `src/debug/api/core.ts`: Added `saturation?: number` support to `setVitals` and included `saturation` and `burning` in `getPlayer()`.
- Tests:
  - `tests/unit/m11a-survival.test.ts`: 33 unit tests covering real `Player.tick()` Appendix D 10-minute sprint scenario on flat floor, action hooks (jump 0.05, sprint-jump 0.2, swimming 0.01 per 3D block, block break 0.005), creative-mode immunity, block use eating prevention, fast/slow regen, starvation floor, eating values, canceling, food item switching, full hunger check, all hazard intervals and rates, suffocation differentiation, water extinguishment over lava, reset(), saturation clamping, and dead flag.
  - `tests/e2e/m11.spec.ts`: 2 end-to-end tests validating 400-tick drowning cycle (air > 0 at tick 299, air 0 at tick 300, health 20 at tick 319, health 18 at tick 320, 2 half-hearts/sec damage, surface refill) and 40-tick lava damage, burning status, and water extinguishment.

## Known limitations

- Death screen UI, bed respawn, and item entity dropping upon death are owned by M11b. In M11a, `dead` flag is set when health reaches 0.
- Attack exhaustion (0.1) will be integrated with the combat system in M13c.
- Night vision effect from Glowcap Stew is deferred until status effect infrastructure is introduced in M20 (Hollowdeep).
