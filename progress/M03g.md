## Investigation: what drew the dark triangles

Method (all probes were throwaway scripts outside the repository, and one `tests/unit/zz-probe-*.test.ts` that was deleted before committing): read the GPU vertex and index buffers back and decode every triangle; project triangles onto chosen pixels and sort by depth; render the normals as colours with a patched fragment shader (the repository was not touched); a controlled block-removal experiment; compare every opaque quad of 1,181 sections with the block data.

- **Desert terraces and forest stone slope: real side faces.** For example the −Z face of the sand block (74, 84, −219): the nearest triangle at a dark pixel is that face, and the other half of it is hidden by the top face of the neighbouring raised sand column (x = 73). Removing three neighbouring sand blocks turned the triangle into the full wall. The mesh equals the block data exactly (no stray and no missing face, correct tiles, planar quads, one normal per quad). They looked black because `FS_CHUNK` computed `max(dot(normal, lightDir), 0.35)`: a top face 0.78, −X and −Z faces the floor 0.35, so 45 % of a top face, and vertex AO is a constant 0. The forest spikes are −X faces of stone blocks at that floor.
- **Water: translucent side walls at the edge of the loaded area.** The padded copy fills a column that is not loaded with air, so the water at the edge of the meshed area got walls toward a column that did not exist (ocean viewpoint of the time: 383 of 823 translucent side quads, about 4,700 of 6,000 square blocks of wall). Seen through the unsorted translucent sea surface they are the dark polygons with the stepped lower edge that follows the seabed. I could not regenerate the old M03c savanna image itself (the generator has changed since); the effect and the radius-2 mesh of that test fit it, but that is not proven for that image.
- **Found on the way:** every +X and −X cube quad was wound clockwise seen from outside (148,776 of about 454,000 triangles); and 9,480 hidden faces stayed in the meshes of the first loaded area after its neighbours were generated.

## The fixes (owner-sanctioned changes to other tasks' code)

Each is one commit and each has a record in `decisions/`. All existing tests pass unchanged after each one (a full `npm run verify` was run after each; unit 199 passed, e2e 28 / 30 / 30 passed at the three points).

### A. Cube-face winding — `src/mesh/greedy.ts` (`emitQuad`), code owned by M02b — `decisions/M03g-face-winding.md`

- **What:** the triangle indices of +X and −X quads are written in reverse order, `(0,2,1),(0,3,2)` instead of `(0,1,2),(0,2,3)`. Vertices, UVs, tiles, tints and merging are byte-identical; only those index arrays change. Cross models keep both windings; back-face culling is not enabled.
- **Why:** all six directions should be counter-clockwise seen from outside, so that culling can be switched on later.
- **Deviation from the prompt ("fix the vertex order"):** my first version swapped corners 1 and 3 (positions and UVs together). Two existing M02b-fix tests (`tests/unit/greedy.test.ts`, "1x3x1 grass_block column …" and "X-, Y-, and Z-axis log bark ridge orientation …") read the UVs by corner index and failed, so I reverted it before committing and reversed the index order instead, which keeps corner identities and passes every existing test unchanged. The result has the same geometry and the same orientation.
- **Test:** `tests/unit/winding.test.ts` (2 tests): every triangle of the opaque, cutout and translucent buckets, in the sections of a 5 × 5 area of generated columns and in a random mixed section (stone, leaves, water, glass, X / Y / Z logs), is counter-clockwise for all six directions, with at least one quad per direction and bucket checked. Both fail on master (`opaque +X triangle 0: cross·normal = -1`) and pass now.

### B. Mesh perimeter — `src/world/world-manager.ts` (`loadInitialWorld`, `meshRadius`), code owned by M02c and M03b-fix3 — `decisions/M03g-mesh-perimeter.md`

- **What:** `PERIMETER_RING = 1`. `createWorld` generates and lights radius 5 (121 columns) and meshes radius 4; `meshRadius(r)` generates every missing column within `r + 1` of the camera and meshes only `r`. `waitForTerrain`'s contract is unchanged; the M03b-fix3 epoch guards are untouched (the extra columns go through the same generation callback). `padded.ts` is unchanged.
- **Why:** a meshed section must always see real neighbour columns, otherwise water gets walls toward a column that does not exist.
- **Test:** `tests/e2e/m03g-perimeter.spec.ts` (2 tests, both fail on master): after a default `createWorld`, and after `waitForTerrain(2)` at another place, no side face of any opaque, cutout or translucent bucket lies on a section border toward a column that `World.hasColumn` reports as not generated; the second test also asserts that every column within the requested radius is generated and meshed.
- **Default `createWorld` duration** (normal frame loop, three fresh pages, standard seed): before 1,742 / 1,700 / 1,748 ms, after 2,169 / 2,034 / 2,021 ms (limit asserted in `tests/e2e/m02c-fix.spec.ts`: 20 s). `worldHash(0,0,64,64)` is `dda64d20` before and after. Draw calls 864 → 841.
- **Stale hidden faces:** gone. The desert scene (1,181 sections) had 9,480 opaque faces toward a neighbour that is opaque in the finished world; it has 0 now, and the meshed unit faces equal the expected ones exactly (551,613 each, none missing).

### C. Face shading — `src/render/chunk-renderer.ts` (`FS_CHUNK`, two lines), code owned by M02c — `decisions/M03g-face-shading.md`

- **What:** the clamped directional diffuse is replaced by a fixed face-shade table: top 1.0, ±Z 0.8, ±X 0.6, bottom 0.5. Nothing else in the shader changed.
- **Why:** the darkest face goes from 0.35 to 0.5, and the shading no longer depends on a light direction.
- **After the fix** (`docs/screenshots/M03g/after-fixes-m03f-desert.png`, `…-forest.png`, rendered by the existing M03f spec; I opened both): the triangles are still there, because they are real geometry, but the desert ones are now a lighter tan that reads as shaded sand, and the forest spikes are a mid-dark grey instead of black (pixel sample of the first spike: (39, 40, 42) before, (67, 69, 72) after; surrounding stone top (77, 80, 84) before, (98, 102, 107) after).
- No unit or e2e test measures this; the existing screenshot-based tests (hue, variance, channel differences) pass unchanged.

### D. Fractional coordinates — `src/world/world.ts` (`getBiome`) and `src/debug/api/locate.ts` (`locateBiome`), code owned by M03c — `decisions/M03g-fractional-coordinates.md`

- **What:** `World.getBiome` floors `x` and `z` before the lookup; `locate('biome', …)` starts from the block that contains `near` (`Math.floor`, it used `Math.round`).
- **Why:** the debug API takes block coordinates, and later tests will pass the player's position, which is fractional. On a loaded column `getBiome(x + 0.5, z + 0.5)` returned `plains` for every biome (`col.biomes[idx] || 'plains'` with a fractional index), so the bug was invisible wherever the real biome was plains. `locate('biome')` rounded, so from x.5 it started one block east or south of the block that holds the position (for example from (1120.5, −446.5) it answered for (1121, −446) instead of (1120, −447)).
- **`getHeight` has no problem** (checked on master with nine positions, including negative ones and fractions 0.25, 0.5 and 0.99): the section lookup truncates the fractional local coordinate to the same block, so `getHeight`, and also `getBlock`, already answer for the containing block. I did not change `getHeight` and left `getBlock` and `setBlock` alone; the new test covers `getHeight`.
- **Test:** `tests/unit/fractional-coordinates.test.ts` (15 tests). For the camera column of each of the seven viewpoints (plains, mountains, ocean, desert, snowy, cave, border; six have a negative x or z, four have both negative), it asserts that `getBiome(x + f, z + f)` for f = 0.25, 0.5 and 0.99, and at the camera position itself, equals `getBiome(x, z)` and the expected biome; that `getHeight` agrees; and that `locate('biome', …)` from (x + 0.5, z + 0.5) equals the answer from (x, z). On master it fails 13 of 15 tests (`getBiome f=0.25: expected 'plains' to be 'frost_peaks'`, the same for ocean, desert, snowy and so on; and the six `locate` ones); the two that pass on master are the plains view's `getBiome` and `getHeight` test (plains is the fallback value, and `getHeight` was never wrong) and the sanity test that the viewpoints include negative coordinates.

## Known limitations

- **No debug light in the cave test.** The chunk shader applies no world light (only the face-shade table), so caves are not dark yet and there is nothing for a debug light to switch. I added no switch.
- **Water is fully opaque** (the tile has no alpha) until M08b: the seabed is visible only from below the surface.
- **Floating overhang fragments** (M03b's 3D density) appear as small loose pieces and, in the frost-peaks massif, as whole floating islands with grass and trees; they are visible near the ridge of `m03-mountains.png` as two tiny fragments. Not changed (generation is out of scope).
- The dark triangles are still in the screenshots: they are real geometry, now lighter.
- `waitForTerrain` now generates (not meshes) one more ring than requested, so a world holds more columns than before; `chunksLoaded` in `getRenderStats()` counts uploaded sections only and is unaffected.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- **M05b:** keep the face-shade table of `FS_CHUNK` (top 1.0, ±Z 0.8, ±X 0.6, bottom 0.5) and multiply it by world light and AO; do not replace it with a new diffuse term. M03f's note to check the dark triangles against AO quad flipping is moot: the cause was flat face shading plus mesh-edge water walls, both fixed here (shading is flat per quad and does not depend on the triangle diagonal).
- **M05b, cave viewpoint:** when world light reaches the shader, add a debug light (a switch that raises the light level for a screenshot) and switch `tests/e2e/m03.spec.ts` › "underground cave viewpoint" to it; today the cave is lit like the surface and nothing is dark.
- **M05b / M22a, culling:** all six cube-face directions are now counter-clockwise seen from outside, so `gl.enable(gl.CULL_FACE)` is safe for cube faces. Cross models (plants) are double-sided and keep both windings, so they need culling off (or a separate draw).
- **M04a, perimeter rule:** a section must never be meshed while any of the eight neighbour columns of its column is missing (`buildPaddedSection` still fills a missing column with air, so the mesher would emit faces toward it, and later-generated neighbours do not remove them). Generate the ring first and mesh inside it; when a column arrives after the sections next to it, re-mesh those sections (or hold their meshing back). `world-manager.ts` has `PERIMETER_RING = 1` for the current load path.
- **M08b:** the water tile is opaque today; when water gets alpha, `m03-ocean.png` can move to a view from above, and the translucent pass is unsorted (back-to-front sorting is M08b's).
- **Viewpoints:** the seven views of this task are good fixed fixtures for later screenshot comparisons (M05b lighting, M08b water, M12 sky); change a hard-coded camera only with the owner's agreement.
