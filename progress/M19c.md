## What was built

- Three original structures registered through the M19a pipeline. `STRUCTURE_REGISTRATIONS` in
  `src/gen/structures/registrations.ts` is used by the generation worker, the main-thread
  fallback and the Create World Structures toggle (off = none).
  - Sunken Crypts (`sunken-crypt.ts`): a buried 11×7×11 mossy hall with a spawner and two chests,
    branching into corridors, tombs (with a chest) and collapses.
  - Spire Ruins (`spire-ruin.ts`): a 20-tall crenellated tower with a chest, linked walls and
    stumps, decayed by noise. Placed in stony_heights and frost_peaks.
  - Mineshaft Warrens (`mineshaft-warren.ts`): a buried timber hub with a chest, plus framed
    tunnels, crossings, stores and cave-ins, with decorative rails and Silkmesh. Up to 14 pieces.
- Stage additions in `stage.ts`, all optional so hamlets are unchanged:
  - `buried` start heights, also returned by `locate structure`;
  - `decay`;
  - per-structure `lootTable`;
  - `spawnerCreatures`;
  - structure markers;
  - no buried structures in flat worlds.
- Spawner block entity (`src/gameplay/spawners.ts`): state `{creature, delay}`, plus the
  activation, darkness, cap and delay rules. Spawns go through `MobRuntime.spawn`. A miniature is
  drawn by the entity renderer for spawners within 32 blocks.
- New blocks: `spawner`, `silkmesh` and `decorative_rail` (axis x|z), each with a generator, an icon
  and drops.
- Chest tables: `data/loot/chests/sunken_crypt.json`, `spire_ruin.json` and
  `mineshaft_warren.json`.

## Changes to other tasks' code

- `src/world/world-manager.ts` (M19b): M19b's per-section chest scan is replaced by
  `applyStructureMarkers`, so each structure's chests roll their own table. Hamlet chests still roll
  `wayfarer_hamlet`. Also passes spawner miniatures to the entity renderer.
- `src/gen/structures/wayfarer-hamlet.ts` (M19b): exports `TemplateBuilder` and sets
  `lootTable: 'wayfarer_hamlet'`.
- `src/gen/structures/stage.ts` and `src/gen/pipeline.ts` (M19a): optional registration fields,
  markers and `takeStructureMarkers`.
- `src/workers/gen.worker.ts` and `gen-worker-pool.ts`: pass markers along.
- `src/game/commands/structure-locate.ts` (M19a): the buried start's y.
- `src/world/block-entities.ts`: optional `nextTickDelay` hook.
- `src/entity/ai/runtime.ts` (M13): a module-level `WeakMap` lookup (`mobRuntimeForWorld`) and a
  `spawningEnabled` getter. No new properties on World or Player.
- `src/entity/ecs/pose.ts` and `model.ts`, and `src/render/entities.ts` (M13): `computeRestPose`
  (shares `writeParts` with `computePose`), `hasEntityModel`, and the miniature draw.
- `src/entity/physics.ts` and `src/entity/player.ts`: the Silkmesh slow.
- `src/gameplay/mining.ts`: the `shears/sword` tool class.
- `src/world/blocks/registry.ts`: axis-specific rail face textures.
- `tests/unit/m10a-reachability.test.ts`: exemptions for `spawner` (drops nothing), `silkmesh`
  (drops silk) and `decorative_rail` (structure-only). This follows M19b's precedent for farmland and
  wheat.

## Known limitations

- **Buried Caches are not done** (a stretch goal, skipped per the owner's instruction).
- Spawners follow the world's mob-spawning option: with spawning off, they never spawn.
- Silkmesh's slow applies to the player only, and not while flying. Mobs walk through it at normal
  speed.
- Rails are decorative only: no carts.

## Notes for dependent tasks

- **`data/block-state-ids.json` was appended after 819:** spawner 820, silkmesh 821,
  decorative_rail[axis=x] 822, decorative_rail[axis=z] 823.
- To add a structure, add a registration to `STRUCTURE_REGISTRATIONS` and a placement entry.
  `lootTable` and `spawnerCreatures` make its chests and spawners work through markers. Do not add
  per-section block scans.
- `buried` registrations are skipped in flat worlds.
- Spawner tuning constants are exported from `src/gameplay/spawners.ts`.
