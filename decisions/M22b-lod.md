# M22b — LOD and loading screen

- The owner chose to default **Distant terrain (LOD)** to off. The Video-tab toggle is persisted in the existing global settings record. Old or damaged records resolve to off. The live setting re-plans streaming and disabling it releases worker jobs and the GPU quad store. LOD runs in local Overworlds above RD12. Hollowdeep, multiplayer, and multiplayer join retain their existing terrain and loading paths.
- The full-detail plan uses radius 12 with the existing generation perimeter and retention hysteresis. The outer range remains Chebyshev distance, and fog still uses `(RD - 1) * 16`.
- Workers use `sampleTerrainTopY` and `sampleBiome` at an absolute cell grid. Top quads are horizontal and side quads extend vertically to lower neighbours. Adjacent tops merge within a column only when height and surface material agree. Tile-edge skirts and column-edge skirts at the full-detail handoff close gaps. The fixed-width tile is 64 blocks and chooses its level from its nearest column. Steps are one block through radius 16, two through radius 24, and four beyond that. The one-column hysteresis band retains the previous level near each boundary. A tile keeps its old mesh visible while its replacement uploads.
- The existing biome surface block supplies top and side atlas tiles, with grass tint on the top and grass fringe. UVs repeat once per block. The shader samples the padded atlas mipmaps and blends to a tile average when the repeat is smaller than a pixel. Lighting uses terrain face shade, sky brightness, brightness setting, and sky fog.
- Water uses an animated translucent surface at the voxel-water source height over opaque seabed. Water quads follow the tile grid so neighbouring cells share wave vertices. The sub-block displacement fades before the first mixed-resolution edge, while the water atlas animation continues at every distance. Both renderers share the terrain fragment shader, animation clock, opaque depth capture and absorption and Fresnel equations. Because height samples and the seabed omit terrain features, water depth remains an approximation outside the one-block ring.
- The shared integer GPU store holds packed quad records. The vertex shader expands each record into two triangles. Workers' sampling time is recorded per level. Visible tiles submit separately in the opaque and translucent passes, using multi-draw when available. Uploads are split into small row segments and use fenced pixel-unpack buffers. If the next buffer is still in use its upload is deferred. Shader compilation and link, texture storage, each staging buffer, and uniform discovery advance in separate frames when at least 2 ms remains of the shared 3 ms deadline. Texture storage and staging buffers can be allocated while the link fence is pending. A zero-timeout fence poll precedes the driver status query. The shader link is polled when `KHR_parallel_shader_compile` is available. Tile transfers start after initialization. Both setup and transfer are included in `uploadMs`, and disabling LOD also releases partially initialized objects.
- The full-detail column mask keeps the LOD visible until a completed voxel mesh is drawn. It clears when the column leaves the detail radius or unloads, and does not require a tile rebuild.
- Create World and Load World use the pixel-art panel and existing initial terrain barrier. Ready-column progress is monotonic and the panel closes before the call resolves.
- Cave visibility keys on camera FOV and aspect.

## Known limitations

- LOD omits trees, structures, cave openings and player edits. Coarse grids approximate coastlines and biome edges.
- Hollowdeep and multiplayer use full detail. Multiplayer join uses its existing loading flow.
